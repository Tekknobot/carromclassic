CARROM - MOBILE PULL-BACK CONTROLS

Mobile portrait only:
1. Move the striker along the glowing baseline.
2. Press PULL BACK under the board.
3. Drag backward (down for an upward shot; left/right to steer).
4. Drag further for greater power, then RELEASE to shoot.
5. A very short drag cancels. No timed hold or automatic 100% shot.

The gesture is captured by one finger. An undelivered touch release is
cancelled after five seconds of inactivity, or when the browser loses focus.

Desktop mode, game rules, physics, sound, both board sets and CPU are unchanged.
