## Full description

CARROM CLASSIC brings two tabletop games into one focused, touch-friendly experience.

Play Classic Carrom or switch to Duboo, choose from eight rival personalities, and test your aim against Casual, Standard or Expert CPU opponents. Discover distinct board designs, line up precise shots and chase the red Queen.

- Classic Carrom and Duboo game modes
- Eight opponents with individual playing styles
- Casual, Standard and Expert difficulty
- Multiple board and table designs
- Shot guide and dedicated touch controls
- Optional spectator Auto Battle
- Offline single-player gameplay with no ads or subscriptions

A traditional board-game experience, wherever you are.
