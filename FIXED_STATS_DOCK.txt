CARROM - Fixed Width Left Stats Panel

- Desktop GAME STATS panel stays 244 px wide even when match/status text changes.
- Left and right panels contain their scrollable content in clipping hosts that
  isolate the panel's minimum size from variable-width labels.
- Long CPU status headings wrap rather than pushing into the board.
- Board diameter is based on the actual clear lane with a small safety gap,
  rather than enforcing an overlapping minimum width at narrow window sizes.
- All three CPU difficulty levels, game rules, modes, and physics are unchanged.

Patch source: user-supplied scripts/game.gd; packaged atop carrom_cpu_refined.zip.
